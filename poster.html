Abstract

LearnIQ is an AI-powered educational platform designed to improve academic retention and performance by analysing attendance cues, predicting CGPA trajectories, detecting early drop-out risks under the 75% GTU rule, adapting personalised study roadmaps, and applying safety checks to encourage helpful and responsible learning.

Problem Statement

Traditional college management portals are static and retrospective—failing to detect early attendance shortages, lacking automated grade risk alerts, and offering zero personalised remediation before final GTU examinations.
